## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a calm, cerebral, and ergonomic workspace for cloud infrastructure engineering and architecture consulting. It balances the structural clarity of neo-brutalism with the softness of warm editorial paper. 

Visual characteristics:
- **Atmosphere:** Low-strain, tactile, deliberate, and quiet. It replaces blinding monitor glare and harsh hyper-saturated cyber palettes with sun-bleached newsprint, matte paper grain, and architectural line work.
- **Core Aesthetic:** Gentle Neo-Brutalist Minimalism. High-contrast structural boundaries are retained via crisp, ultra-thin outlines and subtle physical hard shadows (2px–3px offset), while eliminating aggression through muted, grounding mineral hues.
- **Emotional Intent:** Reduces decision fatigue during complex multi-cloud audits, financial evaluations (denominated in Indian Rupee ₹), and automated refactoring dialogues.

## Colors
The palette is calibrated for long-form viewing under mixed indoor lighting. It rejects stark pure whites (`#FFFFFF`) and absolute pitch blacks (`#000000`) in favor of organic paper fibers and deep charcoal ink.

- **Background Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas (`#F7F4EE`): Warm natural paper base.
  - Surface Elevation 1 / Neutral Light (`#EFECE6`): Recessed containers, muted card backings, and alternating table rows.
  - Surface Elevated Highlight (`#FAF9F5`): Card surfaces requiring active focus or user input.
- **Ink & Structure:**
  - Neutral Primary (`#1E232A`): Deep slate ink for structural borders, headings, and critical labels.
  - Neutral Muted (`#565E69`): Secondary body text, timestamps, and metadata.
- **Functional Accents:**
  - Primary (`#2D5A46`): Forest sage/olive. Reserved for active conversational states, successful optimization metrics, primary actions, and validated infrastructure states.
  - Secondary (`#D97746`): Muted warm terracotta. Applied to high-impact callouts, cost anomalies, architectural alerts, and system cautions.
  - Tertiary (`#4A6B82`): Slate blue. Anchors multi-cloud networking metrics, telemetry tags, and secondary action paths.

## Typography
Typographic scale relies on pairing the technical cadence of **Space Grotesk** with the balanced neutrality of **Hanken Grotesk**.

- **Display & Headings:** Space Grotesk sets structural headings, key cloud compute diagnostics, and financial readouts. Its technical shapes reinforce precise data reporting without visual harshness.
- **Body & Dialogue:** Hanken Grotesk provides neutral, humanized pacing for conversational advisor responses, contextual explanations, and analysis summaries.
- **Financial & Metrics Treatment:** Currency values (₹) and cloud resource tallies always use Space Grotesk with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve column alignment across cloud expenditure reports.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an airy, structured grid that honors horizontal and vertical rhythm.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views utilize an asymmetric 12-column layout. The advisor conversational panel typically reserves a fixed or 5-column wide canvas, with the cloud telemetry and asset breakdown occupying the remaining 7 columns.
- **Canvas Margins & Gaps:** Outer canvas margins (`margin`: 2.5rem on desktop, 1rem on mobile) ensure the system never feels compressed against viewport edges.
- **Breathing Room:** Complex financial projections and AWS/GCP/Azure resource topologies receive expanded internal padding (`space-xl` and `space-lg`) to balance the tactile border lines with open paper negative space.

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with softened neo-brutalist principles, depth is constructed via tactile hard offsets rather than blurred diffuse shadows.

- **Hard 2px/3px Drop Offsets:** Floating panels, actionable cards, and active input bars apply a crisp offset shadow of `2px 2px 0px #1E232A` or `3px 3px 0px #1E232A`. No blur radius is applied (`box-shadow: 2.5px 2.5px 0px 0px #1E232A`).
- **Interactive State Displacement:** On hover, primary interactive cards elevate to `4px 4px 0px #1E232A` with a matching negative translate (`translate(-1px, -1px)`). On active press, the offset resets to `0px 0px 0px #1E232A` (`translate(2.5px, 2.5px)`), delivering physical feedback reminiscent of mechanical keys.
- **Tonal Layering:** Grounding layers shift between `#F7F4EE` (base viewport) and `#EFECE6` (docked sidebars, conversational tracks), framing active cards with soft contrast before borders are even introduced.

## Shapes
The shape language uses `roundedness: 1` (Soft: 0.25rem / 4px base radius, scaling to 8px for outer containers) to soften the severe edge of classic brutalism while remaining geometric and architectural.

- **Component Corners:** 4px radius on buttons, badges, inputs, and internal code tabs prevents sharp corners from puncturing the reading flow.
- **Major Surfaces:** Modals and cloud resource summary cards use `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px).
- **Consistent Boundary:** Every surface edge maintains a consistent 1px or 1.5px structural border rendered in `#1E232A` or `#2D5A46`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#2D5A46` background with warm paper text (`#F7F4EE`), 1.5px border in `#1E232A`, and a `2.5px 2.5px 0px #1E232A` offset. On press, depresses flat with zero shadow.
- **Secondary / Neutral:** Crisp `#FAF9F5` background, `#1E232A` text, 1.5px border in `#1E232A`, and a `2.5px 2.5px 0px #1E232A` offset.
- **Alert / Action:** `#D97746` background with `#FAF9F5` text, used exclusively for cloud cost deprecations, teardown actions, and critical infra commits.

### Conversational Chat Area
- **Advisor Bubble:** Framed container in `#FAF9F5` with a 1px border in `#1E232A` and a subtle `2px 2px 0px #1E232A` shadow. Monospaced metadata tags for query tokens and model confidence.
- **User Bubble:** Muted `#EFECE6` surface, 1px border in `#1E232A`, no offset shadow, aligning to the right with clean body typography.
- **Prompt Input Field:** Contained floating dock at bottom viewport with `#FAF9F5` background, 1.5px `#1E232A` border, `3px 3px 0px #1E232A` offset shadow, and a fixed shortcut hint (`⌘ + ↵`).

### Cards & Cloud Resource Containers
- **Architecture & Metric Cards:** Surface `#FAF9F5`, 1.5px solid `#1E232A` outline, `2px 2px 0px #1E232A` shadow offset, and internal padding of `space-lg`.
- **Cost Metrics & Currency:** Bold display of Indian Rupee pricing (`₹42,850/mo`) with delta tags styled as muted terracotta chips (`+14.2%`) or forest sage chips (`-22.5%`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Small 4px-radius badges with 1px border. Backgrounds use desaturated tints of the functional accents (e.g., `#2D5A46` at 12% opacity with solid `#2D5A46` text).
- **Cloud Provider Tags:** Distinctive bordered tags (AWS, Azure, GCP) set in `Space Grotesk` uppercase (`label-sm`), providing clean visual categorization without loud external brand assets.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** `#FAF9F5` background, 1.5px `#1E232A` border, `space-sm` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding. Focus state adds a `2px 2px 0px #2D5A46` offset ring without blue halos.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp 4px-radius squares (or circles for radio) with a 1.5px `#1E232A` border. Checked states feature solid `#2D5A46` fill and a crisp `#FAF9F5` check indicator.
